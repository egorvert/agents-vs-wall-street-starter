# Fiscal Q2 results and Q3 guidance

## Verified Q2 facts

Analog Devices reported fiscal Q2 2026 revenue of **$3.623 billion**, up **37%** year over year. Adjusted gross margin was **$2.645 billion**, or **73.0% of revenue**, versus 69.4% a year earlier. Adjusted operating margin was **49.0%** and adjusted diluted EPS was **$3.09**.

Industrial revenue was **$1.799 billion**, representing 50% of total revenue and increasing 56% year over year. Automotive revenue was **$872 million**, 24% of revenue and 2% higher year over year. Management said it saw record bookings across its Industrial, Automotive and Communications end markets.

## Official Q3 outlook

For fiscal Q3 2026, management forecast:

- revenue of **$3.9 billion, plus or minus $100 million**;
- reported operating margin of approximately **39.0%, plus or minus 150 basis points**;
- adjusted operating margin of approximately **49.0%, plus or minus 100 basis points**;
- reported EPS of **$2.60, plus or minus $0.15**; and
- adjusted EPS of **$3.30, plus or minus $0.15**.

The company stated that this outlook superseded its prior business-outlook statements.

## Forecast relevance

Revenue and adjusted EPS are directly guided challenge metrics. The adjusted gross-margin challenge metric was **not explicitly guided** in the headline Q3 outlook. The agent should bridge from Q2's 73.0% using revenue leverage, end-market mix, pricing, manufacturing utilization and acquisition-related classification, and should expose that bridge rather than invent a management range.

## Cautions

- Keep GAAP and adjusted results separate.
- ADI's fiscal Q3 timing differs from calendar Q3.
- The $3.9 billion midpoint is guidance, not an actual result.
- Adjusted acquisition-related charges are excluded under ADI's non-GAAP definitions.
