# Analog Devices completes Empower Semiconductor acquisition

Analog Devices announced on 7 July that it had completed its acquisition of Empower Semiconductor. Management described the transaction as expanding ADI's capabilities in high-density, energy-efficient power delivery for AI compute and its addressable market across grid-to-core power systems.

## Forecast relevance

The closing occurred during fiscal Q3 and is strategically relevant to AI-related communications and power demand. However, the release did not quantify near-term acquired revenue, costs or Q3 financial contribution. The agent should not add an arbitrary acquisition increment to revenue or margin.

Acquisition-related accounting also matters because ADI's adjusted gross margin and adjusted EPS exclude specified acquisition-related expenses. Any forecast treatment must follow the company's reconciliation definitions.
