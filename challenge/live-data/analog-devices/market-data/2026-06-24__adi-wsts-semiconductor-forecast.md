# WSTS Spring 2026 semiconductor forecast

WSTS projected the global semiconductor market to reach approximately **$1.51 trillion in 2026**, a 90% increase, led overwhelmingly by memory and AI infrastructure. Within that forecast, **analog semiconductors were expected to grow 10%**, far below the projected memory growth of roughly 250% and logic growth of 37%.

## Forecast relevance

The split is more informative for ADI than the market headline. It supports a strong semiconductor backdrop while warning that the most spectacular market growth is concentrated outside traditional analog. ADI can still benefit from industrial recovery and AI-related power and communications content, but a portfolio-specific model is required.

## Cautions

- WSTS is an industry forecast, not a company forecast.
- Calendar-year category growth is not comparable with ADI's fiscal Q3 growth.
- Revenue mix and margin vary significantly across semiconductor categories.
