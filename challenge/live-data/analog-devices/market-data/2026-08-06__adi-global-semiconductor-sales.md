# June and Q2 global semiconductor sales

## Verified facts

The Semiconductor Industry Association reported that worldwide semiconductor sales were **$134.5 billion in June 2026**, increasing **9.7% month over month** and **123.6% year over year**. Calendar Q2 sales were **$403.3 billion**, up **35.1%** from Q1.

The SIA figures use World Semiconductor Trade Statistics data and describe the global market rather than Analog Devices specifically.

## Forecast relevance

The data confirm very strong aggregate semiconductor demand during ADI's fiscal Q3 window and support management's commentary about record demand and bookings. They are most useful as directional corroboration for revenue strength.

## Cautions

- Aggregate growth is heavily influenced by memory, AI accelerators and other categories that have different economics from analog semiconductors.
- The data do not determine ADI's customer inventory, shipment timing, product mix or adjusted gross margin.
- Do not apply the global growth rate to ADI revenue. Use it as a regime and direction indicator only.
