## Current read-through

Deere maintained FY2026 net-income guidance of $4.5–5.0 billion after Q2, while forecasting Production & Precision Ag annual net sales down 5–10%. July equipment data then showed renewed year-over-year declines in U.S. tractors and combines. USDA's farm-income forecast is mixed: nominal cash income improves, but real net farm income and working capital weaken. These factors argue for an explicit downside/seasonality bridge rather than carrying Q2 results straight into Q3.
