# FTC right-to-repair settlement

On 8 July, the Federal Trade Commission and five states announced a settlement in their antitrust case against Deere. The settlement requires Deere to provide farmers and independent repair providers access to repair resources, including applicable software capabilities, on terms intended to provide parity with authorised dealers. The obligations run for ten years, subject to court entry and oversight.

## Forecast relevance

The development is material to Deere's aftermarket and dealer ecosystem, but the FTC case summary does not quantify a Q3 revenue, cost or profit effect. It should therefore be recorded as a regulatory scenario and potential long-term service-economics factor, not converted into an unsupported Q3 adjustment.

## Cautions

- The case page listed the matter as pending at retrieval, with a stipulated order awaiting court treatment.
- Do not infer a cash charge from the FTC summary.
- The challenge metric is Production & Precision Ag operating profit; any impact may fall elsewhere or emerge over a longer period.
