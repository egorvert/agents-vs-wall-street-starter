# Q2 results and maintained fiscal 2026 guidance

## Verified Q2 facts

Deere reported worldwide net sales and revenues of **$13.369 billion**, up **5%** year over year, and GAAP diluted EPS of **$6.55**. First-half net sales and revenues were **$22.981 billion** and diluted EPS was **$8.97**.

Production & Precision Agriculture Q2 net sales were **$4.503 billion**, down **14%**, while operating profit was **$706 million**, down **39%**, for a 15.7% operating margin. Deere attributed the profit decline primarily to lower shipment volumes and higher production costs, partly offset by favourable foreign exchange.

## Official outlook

Deere maintained fiscal 2026 net-income guidance of **$4.5 billion to $5.0 billion**. Its fiscal 2026 industry and segment outlook included:

- U.S. and Canadian large agriculture equipment: **down 15% to 20%**;
- South American tractors and combines: **down approximately 15%**;
- Production & Precision Ag net sales: **down 5% to 10%**, including approximately +3% currency translation and +1% price realisation;
- Small Ag & Turf net sales: up approximately 15%; and
- Construction & Forestry net sales: up approximately 20%.

Deere also recorded a **$272 million recovery** for accepted refund claims related to invalidated IEEPA tariffs. Tariff impacts were primarily classified in production costs.

## Forecast relevance

Q2 actuals and the segment outlook directly constrain all three Deere challenge metrics. For Q3, the agent should model segment shipment seasonality, continued PPA volume pressure, production costs, finance revenue and the unusual tariff-recovery comparison separately.

## Cautions

- The challenge EPS metric is GAAP diluted EPS.
- Company net-income guidance is annual and cannot be divided evenly by quarter.
- Worldwide net sales and revenues include Financial Services and other revenues; do not substitute equipment net sales.
