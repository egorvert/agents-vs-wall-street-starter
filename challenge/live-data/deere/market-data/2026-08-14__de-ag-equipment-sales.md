# July U.S. and Canadian agricultural-equipment sales

The Association of Equipment Manufacturers reported that July 2026:

- U.S. agricultural tractor sales fell **10.9%** year over year;
- U.S. combine sales fell **5.3%**;
- Canadian agricultural tractor sales fell **7.8%**; and
- Canadian combine sales fell **10.8%**.

AEM characterised the data as continued softness amid persistent economic uncertainty.

## Forecast relevance

This is the freshest pre-cutoff external demand indicator for Deere's core North American agriculture market and overlaps Deere's fiscal Q3. The broad decline is directionally negative for equipment shipment volume and supports management's cautious large-ag industry outlook.

It is an external check on assumptions for **Worldwide net sales and revenues** and **Production & Precision Ag operating profit**, not a substitute for either company measure.

## Cautions

- AEM reports industry unit sales, not Deere orders, production, revenue or market share.
- Product mix, price, currency, dealer inventory and recognition timing can produce different revenue changes.
- The all-tractor measure includes categories outside Production & Precision Ag.
- Use the data to challenge shipment assumptions, not as a direct percentage adjustment to Deere sales.
