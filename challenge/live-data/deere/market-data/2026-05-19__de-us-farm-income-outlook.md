# USDA 2026 U.S. farm-income forecast

USDA ERS forecast 2026 net farm income at **$153.4 billion**, down **0.7% nominally** and **2.6% after inflation** from 2025. Net cash farm income was forecast at **$158.5 billion**, up **3.0% nominally** and **1.1% in real terms**.

Crop cash receipts were forecast at **$240.8 billion**, up 1.2% nominally, including higher corn receipts. Farm-sector debt was forecast to rise **5.2%** to $624.7 billion, while working capital was expected to decline **9.2%**.

## Forecast relevance

Farm income and liquidity influence producers' ability and willingness to purchase large equipment. The official outlook is mixed: cash income remains above its long-run average, but real profit, leverage and working capital point to constrained discretionary capacity. The combination is consistent with selective replacement demand rather than a broad large-ag upcycle.

This evidence should inform demand scenarios for **Worldwide net sales and revenues** and **Production & Precision Ag operating profit**. It has no direct mechanical mapping to **Diluted EPS (GAAP)**.

## Cautions

- This is a calendar-year U.S. sector forecast and is broader than Deere's global customers.
- Government payments, livestock income and smaller farms affect aggregates differently from large row-crop operators.
- The next USDA farm-income update was scheduled after this archive cutoff.
