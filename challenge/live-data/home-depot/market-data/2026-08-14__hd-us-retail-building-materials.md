# July retail sales for building-material and garden-equipment dealers

## Verified facts

The Census Bureau's 14 August advance retail report estimated July 2026 U.S. retail and food-services sales at **$763.6 billion**, down **0.6%** month over month and up **5.0%** year over year.

For NAICS 444, building-material and garden-equipment and supplies dealers, seasonally adjusted sales were:

- **$42.583 billion** in July 2026;
- **$42.456 billion** in June 2026; and
- **$39.912 billion** in July 2025.

The category therefore increased **0.3% month over month** and **6.7% year over year**. May through July category sales were **6.0%** above the comparable 2025 period.

## Forecast relevance

This is the freshest high-frequency public demand proxy covering much of Home Depot's U.S. assortment. July overlaps the latter part of Home Depot's fiscal Q2 and is directionally supportive for nominal category demand.

## Cautions

- Census data cover the entire category, not Home Depot, and include competitors and different channel mixes.
- The series is nominal and not adjusted for product inflation or deflation.
- Home Depot's fiscal calendar, acquisitions, Canadian/Mexican operations and comparable-store methodology differ from the Census category.
- Advance estimates are sampled and can be revised. Do not map the 6.7% category growth directly to Home Depot comparable sales.
