# Q1 results and reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance

## Verified facts

Home Depot reported first-quarter fiscal 2026 sales of **$41.8 billion**, up **4.8%** year over year. Total-company comparable sales increased **0.6%** and U.S. comparable sales increased **0.4%**. Foreign exchange added approximately **55 basis points** to total-company comparable sales. Adjusted diluted EPS was **$3.43**, versus **$3.56** a year earlier.

Management reaffirmed fiscal 2026 guidance:

- total sales growth of approximately **2.5% to 4.5%**;
- comparable sales growth of approximately **flat to 2.0%**;
- gross margin of approximately **33.1%**;
- operating margin of approximately **12.4% to 12.6%** and adjusted operating margin of **12.8% to 13.0%**;
- GAAP diluted EPS growth of approximately **flat to 4.0%** from fiscal 2025's $14.23; and
- adjusted diluted EPS growth of approximately **flat to 4.0%** from fiscal 2025's $14.69.

The CEO described underlying demand as broadly similar to fiscal 2025 despite consumer uncertainty and housing-affordability pressure.

## Forecast relevance

This is the most authoritative pre-cutoff company guidance for all three Home Depot challenge metrics. Q1 supplies a current run-rate, while the annual ranges constrain reasonable Q2 outcomes. The SRS/GMS contribution and new locations affect total sales more than comparable sales, so those measures must not be forecast with the same growth rate.

## Cautions

- The challenge EPS metric is **adjusted diluted EPS**, not GAAP EPS.
- Comparable sales are percentage points; `1.0` means 1.0%.
- Annual guidance is not a quarterly forecast and should be combined with seasonality and Q2-specific evidence.
