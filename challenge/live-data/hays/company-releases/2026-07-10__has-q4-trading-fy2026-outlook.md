# Q4 trading statement and FY2026 profit outlook

## Verified Q4 facts

For the three months ended 30 June 2026, group net fees declined **5% like for like** and **4% on an actual-currency basis**. Like-for-like regional changes were:

- Germany: **down 7%**;
- UK & Ireland: **down 8%**;
- Australia & New Zealand: **down 2%**; and
- Rest of World: **down 1%**.

Temporary and Contracting net fees declined **3%** like for like, while Permanent declined **7%**. Hays said temporary volumes and average hours worked in Germany were stable, while permanent recruitment remained challenging and softened in several markets.

Consultant net-fee productivity increased **8%**, the eleventh consecutive positive quarter. Hays also said approximately **£50 million of annualised savings** had been secured in FY2026, ahead of its longer-term schedule.

## Official outlook

Management expected FY2026 pre-exceptional operating profit at the **top of the £37.0 million to £46.0 million consensus range**, following strong year-over-year profit growth in the second half.

## Forecast relevance

This is the final company trading update before the FY2026 results and directly constrains operating profit. The regional and segment fee changes also provide the last disclosed run-rate for annual net fees. Productivity and cost savings help explain why profit can improve even while fees decline.

- The statement provides a range position, not a precise reported number.
