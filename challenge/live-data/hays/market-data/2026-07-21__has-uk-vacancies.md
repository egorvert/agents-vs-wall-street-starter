# UK vacancies in April to June 2026

The ONS estimated **712,000 UK vacancies** in April to June 2026, down **7,000 or 0.9%** from January to March and down **18,000 or 2.5%** from a year earlier. Vacancies declined in 10 of 18 industry sectors. There were **2.5 unemployed people per vacancy** in March to May 2026, up from 2.3 a year earlier.

## Forecast relevance

The data overlap Hays' fiscal Q4 and corroborate the company's description of a challenging UK permanent-recruitment market. A softer vacancy stock is directionally negative for recruitment placement volumes and net fees.

## Cautions

- ONS estimates cover the whole UK labour market, not Hays' professional specialisms.
- Three-month averages lag turning points and the quarterly decrease is within the published confidence interval.
- Vacancies are a directional driver, not a direct conversion to Hays net fees.
