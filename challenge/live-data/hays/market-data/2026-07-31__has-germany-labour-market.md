# German labour market in July 2026

The German Federal Employment Agency's July assessment said economic weakness remained visible in the labour market. Seasonally adjusted unemployment increased, underemployment was broadly unchanged, registered labour demand remained persistently low and employment subject to social-insurance contributions continued to decline.

The preceding June release recorded 2.936 million unemployed people, a 6.2% unemployment rate, and a BA-X labour-demand index of 103, with registered vacancies below the prior year.

## Forecast relevance

Germany is Hays' largest regional business. The July release was published after fiscal year-end but before Hays reported results, and it corroborates weak hiring conditions around the Q4 exit rate. It is directionally consistent with Hays' disclosed 7% like-for-like decline in German Q4 net fees.

## Cautions

- July conditions partly fall after Hays' 30 June year-end; use them as an exit-rate check, not as direct FY2026 activity.
- Official economy-wide indicators do not isolate Hays' professional and contracting markets.
- The qualitative July assessment should not be converted into an arbitrary numeric fee adjustment.
