# Australian labour force in June 2026

In seasonally adjusted terms, Australian employment increased by **76,300 people, or 0.5%**, in June to 14.823 million. The unemployment rate remained **4.4%**, participation rose to **67.0%**, and underemployment increased 0.2 percentage points to **6.5%**.

## Forecast relevance

June is the final month of Hays' fiscal year. The employment increase provides a firmer macro backdrop for Australia & New Zealand than the UK and Germany evidence, consistent with Hays reporting a smaller Q4 ANZ net-fee decline on a like-for-like basis.

## Cautions

- Much of the employment gain was part-time and unemployment also increased in level terms.
- Economy-wide employment is not the same as professional recruitment placements or Hays net fees.
- ABS identified a weighting adjustment for parts of the June sample; use the data with the agency's published quality cautions.
